How it works

  1. Buy your challenge through Candor at an eligible partner firm, with code CANDOR or Candor's link.
  2. Log in below with Google (or email).
  3. Send us the firm, your order number and the email you bought with. We find the purchase in our partner account and switch on early access.

Why is it free?

Candor is our comparison site for futures prop firms. When you buy a challenge through it, the firm pays us a commission. Your price is the same, or lower with the code.

That commission pays for the desk, so we give buyers the notes first. Your order number and email are only used to find your purchase.
