Method

How the desk reads NQ.

Waves, read with MACD

We read the market in waves, using our own MACD-based method.

One colour per timeframe

Trendlines on our charts are marked by timeframe. Each timeframe has its own colour, so we always know which one we are talking about.

The two patterns that matter most

For us the most important patterns are the double top and the double bottom.

What we do not do

No entries, stops or targets. No signals, no calls to buy or sell. This is how we read the market, written down as it happens. What you do with it is your decision.

Privacy

What SB Desk keeps, and why.

Accounts

If you create an account (with Google or with email) we keep your email address, so you can log in; with email we also keep a protected password. If you ask for early access we also keep the prop firm and order number you send, only to check that you bought through our Candor code.

Suggestions

If you send a suggestion we keep the text, and a name and contact only if you write them. We read them to improve the desk.

In your browser

Your light or dark choice is saved in your own browser. When you log in, a login cookie keeps you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who handles it

SB Desk is run by Bernard and Stipe Pupačić in Split, Croatia. The site and its data are hosted by Netlify. We do not sell or share your data.
