# Vision

Written 17 Nov 2026 from Stipe's own words in chats (6–17 Nov). Change it only through `decisions.md`.

## What SB Desk is

A serious, long-term project: the public research desk of a father and son who trade NQ (Nasdaq 100 futures) from Split, Croatia. It should feel like an institution, not an entertainment site: it builds the reputation of Bernard and Stipe Pupačić in the prop trading world.

- **Bernard Pupačić**: founder & owner, head of the desk.
- **Stipe Pupačić**: co-founder, trader. Also runs Candor (propfirmcandor.com).

## What makes it different

- **Objective waves.** They read the market in waves with their own MACD-based method. Elliott Wave is subjective: ten people can count the same chart in ten different ways. Here MACD decides where a wave starts and ends, so the reading is objective. This is the core message for people searching for wave analysis.
- **Timestamped, never edited.** Every note keeps its time; nothing is deleted or rewritten. Later a "Before / After" chart can show how it played out: hits and misses stay up the same way. That is the proof, without signals.
- **Four timeframes**, each with its own colour on the charts (1m red, 5m yellow, 15m purple, 1H light blue, 4H dark blue, 1D orange). Charts are posted on 4H, 1H, 15m, 5m and 1m (1m added 17 Nov 2026, e.g. to show a 1m double top).
- The most important patterns for them: **double top and double bottom**.

## Who it is for

Prop traders. Most of the audience is "degen": they do not read analysis unless they see on X that it works. Before / After pictures are the first hook. Stipe will use one personal X account (his name and photo) once he has a funded account.

## The business

- Every note is **free** for everyone.
- People who buy a prop firm challenge **through the Candor code** get **free early access**: they see each new note as soon as it is posted; everyone else 24 hours later.
- Candor shows SB Desk as its main marketing message (a card on the home page), once the partner firms confirm their affiliate terms allow it.
- No signals, no copy trading, no paid tier.

- Gold or other symbols: maybe later. NQ only now.
